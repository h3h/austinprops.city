Proposition C changes the rules for the Independent Citizens Redistricting
Commission, the group of residents (not council members) who draw Austin’s
council district lines. This is a [charter amendment][LC] that the City Council
put on the ballot.

It would:

* Say outright that the commission is independent of City Council control
* Let the commission review districts after the city’s boundaries change, for
  example when the city annexes land
* Let the student member of the commission skip the requirement that every
  commissioner has been registered to vote in Austin for five straight years
* Set when a new district map takes effect

The student rule is the most concrete change. Today the commission has a seat
for a student, but almost no student has lived and voted in Austin for five
years, so the seat is hard to fill ([Daily Texan][DT]).

These are sensible fixes to a system that has worked well since voters created
it in 2012. We found no organized opposition.

**Recommendation:** Yes
