Proposition F makes two changes to the City Charter’s campaign finance rules.
This is a [charter amendment][LC] that the City Council put on the ballot.

* It defines what counts as an “election” for the charter’s campaign finance
  limits.
* It changes when the city adjusts campaign contribution and spending limits
  for inflation. Today the adjustment happens when the council adopts the
  budget. Under Prop F it would happen by January 1 each year, or as state law
  provides.

The limits themselves don’t change. Austin’s current limit is
[$500 per contributor per election][CL], and it would still be adjusted using
the same inflation index. A fixed January date means candidates and donors know
the limit before the election year starts.

**Recommendation:** Yes
