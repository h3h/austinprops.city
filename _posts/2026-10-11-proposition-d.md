Proposition D removes the line in the City Charter that says the City Council
must meet at least once a week. This is a [charter amendment][LC] that the City
Council put on the ballot.

The council already sets its meeting calendar by ordinance, and in practice it
usually meets every other week ([Community Impact][CI]). The charter language doesn’t match how the council
works today.

The council would still have to hold public meetings under the Texas Open
Meetings Act. This changes how often they are required to meet, not whether
the public can see them.

**Recommendation:** Yes
